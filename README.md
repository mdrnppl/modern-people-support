# Modern People - Support

Modern People is a social platform for sharing books, films, and podcasts with friends.

## Contact & Support

For help, questions, or feedback:
- **Email**: team@modernpeople.com

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create an account?
Download the app and sign up with your email. You'll need to complete your profile with a username and profile picture.

### How do I add friends?
Use the Follow Suggestions feature or search for users by username.

### How do I add content to my library?
Search for books, films, or podcasts and tap the bookmark or state buttons to add them to your library.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

### How do I report a bug?
Email us at team@modernpeople.com with:
- Description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Your device and iOS version

### How do I request a feature?
We'd love to hear your ideas! Email us at team@modernpeople.com
